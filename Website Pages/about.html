Biography

I’m a Product Designer with 8+ years of experience building consumer products, enterprise platforms, and AI-powered experiences.

I specialize in driving product strategy and end-to-end design, transforming complex problems into intuitive and scalable experiences. Throughout my career, I’ve worked across 0→1 products, marketplaces, AI-driven experiences, and enterprise systems — defining product direction, designing user journeys, and building scalable design foundations.

I’ve led product design as a Head of Product Design and Founding Product Designer, partnering closely with product, engineering, and business teams to align user needs with strategic goals and deliver impactful products.

My work sits at the intersection of product strategy, systems thinking, and execution. I’m passionate about creating products that simplify complexity, improve decision-making, and scale with growing organizations.
